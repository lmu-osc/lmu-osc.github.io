## Mission Statement

FAIR-by-design data sharing maximizes the value of research investments and accelerates biomedical progress. It will become increasingly important for the visibility and discoverability of research outputs. Achieving interoperability requires community-wide agreement on common data and metadata standards that can be integrated into user-friendly tools and platforms.
