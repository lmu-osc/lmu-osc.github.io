## Overview

This workshop introduces preregistration and registered reports, covering their purpose, key components, and potential to prevent bias in empirical research. We will critically discuss the benefits and limitations of these practices, including potential concerns and barriers to adoption, through a structured academic debate. Participants will also practice assessing the precision of preregistrations through peer review and learn how to preregister a study using the Open Science Framework (OSF).
