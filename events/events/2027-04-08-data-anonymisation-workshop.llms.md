## Overview

This two-day hands-on workshop will introduce participants to legal and practical aspects of sharing research data while protecting privacy and confidentiality. It will cover GDPR compliance and related legal considerations, followed by practical introductions to two approaches: **data anonymization** and **synthetic data generation**.

Participants will work through the main sections of two interactive tutorials, with guidance and demonstrations from experienced instructors: [Luisa Jansen](https://luisajansen.github.io/), [Thom Volker](https://thomvolker.github.io/), [Alyssa Columbus](https://alyssacolumbus.com/), and [Felix Schönbrodt](https://www.nicebread.de/). The workshop will conclude with dedicated time for participants to explore how these techniques could be applied to their own research projects, with opportunities for individual and group support.

**The workshop will cover:**

- **Legal considerations (approximately 1 hour):** GDPR compliance and related legal aspects of research data sharing.

- **Data anonymization (approximately 3 hours):** An introduction to key concepts and practical techniques, working through the [Data Anonymization Tutorial](https://lmu-osc.github.io/data-anonymization/).

- **Synthetic data (approximately 3 hours):** An introduction to synthetic data generation and its applications, working through the [Synthetic Data Tutorial](https://lmu-osc.github.io/synthetic-data-tutorial/).

- **Applying the techniques to your own research (approximately 4 hours):** Dedicated time to explore and experiment with anonymization or synthetic data techniques on participants’ own projects, supported by the instructors.

The workshop combines conceptual introductions, guided practical exercises, and hands-on project work. Participants will have the opportunity to work individually or in pairs and receive feedback and support from the instructors throughout.

**Prerequisites:** R proficiency
