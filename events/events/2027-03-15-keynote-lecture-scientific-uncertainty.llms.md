## Overview

Join an **Evening Lecture** given by **Gerd Gigerenzer** (MPI für Bildungsforschung) - a renowned German psychologist, known for his work around bound rationality, heuristics and decision-making in times of uncertainty. It is the starting point for a Symposium at CAS the next day, which explores the paradox at the intersection between Open Science and science communication and discusses how Bayesian approaches can improve the modelling and communication of scientific evidence and uncertainty.
