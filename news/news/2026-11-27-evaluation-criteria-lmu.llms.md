# Reproducibility Recognized In Academic Recruitment At LMU

November 27, 2026

Alongside coherent and innovative research programmes, rigorous methodology, reproducibility and transparency are vital — if not foundational — in the empirical sciences.

We are delighted to share that the LMU Department of Psychology has adopted a **mission statement** for hiring committees that explicitly **recognizes** good research practices, namely **research transparency and reproducibility**, as criteria for evaluating research profiles.

The statement promotes a multidimensional assessment of academic achievements, moving beyond traditional publication metrics to **recognize diverse contributions to research, teaching, the scientific community or the general public** — such as training of young researchers, roles in relevant professional conferences or science communication.
